# Community code of conduct

Aether is a technical community where people should be able to contribute
without harassment, intimidation, or exclusion.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, respectful, and constructive in reviews and discussions.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Credit sources and disclose relevant conflicts or automated contributions.
- Respect privacy, licenses, trademarks, and coordinated security disclosure.
- Accept a maintainer's request to pause a thread while facts are verified.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing,
spam, impersonation, deliberate sabotage, and knowingly submitting malicious
or unlicensed material are not permitted in project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary disagreements, use the relevant issue or pull request. For a
private conduct report, use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting channel
and begin the report title with `Conduct report:`. Maintainers will limit the
report to people needed to investigate, acknowledge it as soon as practical,
and may edit or remove content, lock threads, reject contributions, or suspend
participation. Retaliation against a reporter is itself a violation.

This policy applies in the repository and in public spaces where someone is
representing Aether or DesignFeed.
